\chapter{Alphabetical Verb Tables}

\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=referenceBlueBg,colframe=genderMasculine]
\textbf{B1 coverage.} The entries marked ``Additional B1 verbs'' complete the
verb headwords in the official
\href{https://www.goethe.de/pro/relaunch/prf/pt/br/Goethe-Zertifikat_B1_Wortliste.pdf}{Goethe-Zertifikat B1 vocabulary list}.
Multiword constructions built from a verb already tabulated (for example
\emph{geboren werden}) are covered by the underlying verb rather than repeated
as a separate conjugation.
\end{tcolorbox}
