# Related verb additions, November 2026

This batch follows the requested tension, prescribing, farewell, driving,
handing-over, restraint and detention vocabulary. The working readings of
the original spellings were `anspannen`, `verschreiben` and `verabschieden`.
`Verschreien` was not added without confirmation of that separate meaning.
The comparative and superlative chapter was added subsequently in
`grammar/comparative-and-superlative.tex`.

## Inventory

Thirty additional full tables bring the book to 630. One of those,
`festhalten`, replaces its former compact entry under `halten`. Four new
compact entries (`einspannen`, `ausspannen`, `anbinden`, `festbinden`) bring
the compact total to 349, giving 979 indexed headwords overall.

| Group | Additional full tables |
| --- | --- |
| Tension and stretching | spannen, anspannen, sich anspannen, entspannen, dehnen, strecken |
| Writing and prescribing | aufschreiben, vorschreiben, verordnen |
| Farewells | sich verabschieden |
| Driving and pursuing | antreiben, betreiben, vertreiben |
| Handing over | überreichen, übergeben, weitergeben, aushändigen, sich übergeben |
| Restraint and restriction | binden, fesseln, hindern, beschränken, einschränken, festhalten |
| Catching, detention and release | erwischen, festnehmen, einsperren, inhaftieren, befreien, freilassen |

The existing full tables for `treiben`, `verschreiben`, `sich verschreiben`,
`verabschieden`, `verhaften` and `zwingen` have clearer meanings or additional
government patterns. Existing `fangen`, `entlassen`, `sich entspannen` and
`übertreiben` remain available alongside the additions.

## Editorial decisions

- `übergeben` and `überreichen` are inseparable in the selected meanings;
  `weitergeben`, `festnehmen`, `festhalten` and `freilassen` are separable.
- `überreichen`, `übergeben`, `weitergeben`, `aushändigen`, `vorschreiben`
  and `verordnen` are purple for the dative recipient and accusative thing.
  The other new tables are blue; a case after a preposition does not change
  that classification.
- `aufschreiben` covers writing down. The additional medical sense is not
  selected for that table. Its freely added beneficiary does not determine
  the background.
- `antreiben` covers driving/powering and spurring on, with `haben`; its
  different drifting/being washed ashore sense with `sein` is outside the
  selected gloss. Likewise, the gloss for `treiben` covers driving and
  pursuing an activity, rather than the intransitive drifting sense.
- `sich übergeben` is kept as its own reflexive table for vomiting. No normal
  imperative is shown; its absence follows the book's dash convention.
- `sich verschreiben` distinguishes a writing error from devoting oneself
  to a cause. The dative cause is now explicit in its government block.
- `verabschieden` distinguishes seeing someone off or adopting legislation
  from the new reflexive `sich verabschieden von + Dat`.
- The passive chapter supplies the requested "being" constructions. State,
  process and location are illustrated separately.

## Follow-up review

The 30 additional tables were checked again for person endings, prefix
placement, participles, reflexive pronouns and government. In particular,
Duden confirms the recorded variants
[`fessle`](https://www.duden.de/konjugation/fesseln) and
[`bandest`](https://www.duden.de/konjugation/binden), and the forms of
[`verordnen`](https://www.duden.de/konjugation/verordnen) and
[`hindern`](https://www.duden.de/konjugation/hindern).

The commercial sense of `vertreiben` is now glossed as "market goods" to
distinguish it from simply handing things out. `Verhaften wegen` is glossed
as "arrest for", without adding "on suspicion of" to the meaning of `wegen`.
The printed block heading is now `KONSTRUKTIONEN`, since its rows include
direct object patterns as well as prepositions; the legend explains this.

At the end of this verb batch, the book contained the existing adjective-ending
tables but no comparison chapter. The subsequent comparison chapter includes
60 adjective examples; see `docs/adjective-comparison-notes.md`.

## Subsequent complete-catalogue audit

The subsequent [linguistic audit](verb-audit-2026-11.md) corrected older entries,
promoted four compact items into complete tables, replaced obsolete standalone
`ahmen` with `nachahmen`, and separated the two common meanings of `schaffen`.
The current totals are 635 complete tables and 334 compact compounds (969
indexed entries). The adjective chapter now has 60 entries side by side.

At the user’s request, `ahmen` was subsequently restored as the full base
table, marked obsolete, with `nach|ahmen` listed beneath it.
